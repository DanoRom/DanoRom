## For TypeScript/Next.js projects

- **Keep dependencies current with `npm outdated`** or Dependabot — Next.js
  major versions move fast, and deferred upgrades get painful.
- **Monitor with Vercel's free Analytics and Speed Insights** — no extra
  service needed for a small project.
- **Run `npm audit` periodically** and fix high-severity issues promptly,
  especially anything touching user input.
- **Revisit `next.config.mjs` and `tsconfig.json`** after major version
  bumps — defaults change, and unused options linger silently.
- **Track Core Web Vitals, not just uptime.** A live-but-slow frontend loses
  users just as surely as a downed one.
