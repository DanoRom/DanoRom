# Stage: Deployment

The app works. Time to put it in front of users without burning money.

## What this stage is about
Getting to production on free tiers, and knowing when production breaks.

## Key activities
- **Choose a free-tier host.** Plenty of platforms run small apps and databases at
  zero cost — verify the tier is genuinely free before committing.
- **Add health checks and logging.** You should learn about outages from dashboards,
  not from users.
- **Polish the repository.** Complete README, intentional license, screenshots.
- **Set up a deploy pipeline.** Merging to main should deploy, or be one click away.

## You're ready for the next stage when
- A stranger can reach the app at a URL.
- You would know within minutes if it went down.
